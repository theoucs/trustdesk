# Service Level Agreement and Support

## Availability commitment

| Plan | Monthly uptime commitment |
|---|---|
| Starter | No contractual commitment |
| Business | 99.9% |
| Enterprise | 99.95% |

Availability is measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance. Actual availability over the last 12 months is published on the status page.

## Service credits

If the monthly uptime falls below the commitment, Business and Enterprise customers are eligible for service credits:

- 10% of the monthly fee if uptime is below the commitment but at least 99.0%.
- 25% of the monthly fee if uptime is below 99.0%.

Service credits must be requested within 30 days after the end of the affected month and are capped at 25% of the monthly fee.

## Scheduled maintenance

Scheduled maintenance takes place on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 (Paris time) for the EU region, and between 02:00 and 06:00 (New York time) for the US region. Customers are notified at least 72 hours in advance. Most releases are deployed with zero downtime and do not require a maintenance window.

## Support

Support is available by email, in-app chat and the help center, in English and French.

| Priority | Business (business hours, Mon–Fri) | Enterprise (24/7 for P1) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 – Service unavailable | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P2 – Major feature impaired | 8 hours | 4 hours |
| P3 – Minor issue or question | 2 business days | 1 business day |

Times are first-response targets, not resolution times. Starter customers have access to the help center and email support on a best-effort basis.

Enterprise customers are assigned a dedicated Customer Success Manager.
